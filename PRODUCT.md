# Propniti

Property management software for US residential and commercial portfolios, built around AI agents that do routine work while people approve anything involving money, legal terms or safety.

## Promise

**Know your portfolio is right.** Propniti continuously checks, explains and corrects the operations data that money decisions depend on.

1. **Data you can trust.** Data Health finds and fixes bad records (duplicates, miscoded expenses, unverified bank changes, rent typos) before owners or auditors do.
2. **Every number explained.** Any KPI or chart opens the rows behind it, and "Explain" answers why a number moved, using figures computed from the data.
3. **Ahead of problems.** Delinquency risk, compliance and insurance expiries, and cash forecasts surface work before it becomes urgent.
4. **What-if.** Rent, occupancy and expense scenarios show the NOI impact per property.

## Users

- **Operators:** principal, regional manager, property manager, leasing agent, broker, accountant, maintenance supervisor and technician, admin.
- **Portals:** owners, residents, vendors, applicants.
- **Public:** renters browsing listings.

Each person sees only their own scope: their region, properties or records.

## Product principles

- **Serious, dense, calm.** This is enterprise software where real money moves. Use neutral surfaces, one deep-teal accent, Geist with tabular numbers, and sentence-case labels. No gimmicks, gradients or marketing styling inside the app.
- **AI is visible and accountable.** Every AI output shows its source (live model, precomputed, or rules), its reasoning, its confidence, and a link to the run that produced it. Agents propose; people approve money, legal and safety actions.
- **Figures come from data, never from the model.** The model words answers and suggests actions; the numbers are computed.
- **Every number clicks through.** KPIs, charts and totals drill down to the records behind them.
- **Honest copy.** No claims the product doesn't back up, and no invented customers, logos or testimonials. Demo data is labelled as sample data.
- **Fair housing.** Never use protected characteristics in scoring, listings or answers.

## Tone

Plain, specific and professional. Short sentences. Name who does what.
